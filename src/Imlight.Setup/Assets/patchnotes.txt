# Next update (in testing)

Q: New

Arc 3, another boss rush through three new worlds, tiers 11 to 13: Polaris (The Rat, in the Jeweled Slopes), Mirage (Grandfather Spider, in the South Time Dune in the Shadowwock's place) and Empyrea (Medulla, in the Brain).

The Rat gives the experience to reach level 120, and Grandfather Spider to 130, the new cap.

Shops, drops, jewels and trainers now stock up to level 130. Shops also sell gear from all over the game: each world's own drops, the gauntlets' gear (such as the level 130 Vanguard gear from the Catacombs) and the dungeon and boss sets at every level (Malistaire's at 50, Darkmoor's at 100). Every slot now has gear up to 130; before, boots stopped around 106 and rings and athames at 118.

Trainers offer more spells, from level 1 to 130: 15 for each school professor (and the other trainers) and 8 for each of Ravenwood's school trees, even past what they normally teach. Settings has "Spells per trainer" and "Spells per tree".

Achievements for arc 3: The Rat Caught, Web Unwoven, Arc 3 Complete, and At the Summit (reach level 130).

The launcher's map shows all 13 tiers, and Morganthe ends arc 2 with the "continue or take a break" choice.

Q: Fixed

Drops come from your own level: gear, crown gear and jewels that drop (or come from chests) are from your level down to 10 levels under it, never above it, and no longer anything you have the level for (a level 130 wizard got a level 35 jewel). Shops still stock a few levels above you.

A creature's minions fight on its side: Grandfather Spider's spiderlings (and every other creature's summons) came in on the wizards' side. (Fixed in Niduus's fork too.)

No random music in the Shopping District: it plays its own Wizard City tune, so the random music made two songs at once. Random music starts again on the streets and in boss rooms.

# v2.1.0 (October 26, 2026) - Achievement Update

Q: New

Behind the scenes: a test realm. Updates now go to a separate Spiral Gauntlet Test Realm first (its own install, server and wizards) and only reach everyone once they've been tried out, so a broken update shouldn't reach your game.

In the test realm every account is an administrator, so testers can use every command (.run goto, .run pwn, .jewel and the rest).

Achievements! 25 of them, from clearing each world to beating a boss in 3 rounds, finding an Ultra pet or holding a million gold (and two hidden ones). The game tells you when you earn one, the launcher has an Achievements page showing what you've earned and what's left, and .run achievements lists them in game. Achievements stay earned on your PC even if the wizard who earned them is lost to Die = Delete.

The launcher window is a little bigger, so its buttons no longer run into the rest of the window.

Settings has each street's minutes for tiers 1 to 5 (Wizard City 10, the rest 15 to start), under The run.

Q: Fixed

.run status, .run stats and .run cards work for every player. Commands were only read for staff accounts, and the accounts the launcher makes at the login screen aren't staff, so the game treated them as chat.

# v2.0.1 (October 25, 2026)

The launcher's map calls tier 10 "Morganthe" instead of Wintertusk (she fights in a Wintertusk cave for now, since her own room doesn't work yet). Any tier can be renamed on the map with TierNName in Imlight.ini.

# v2.0.0 (October 25, 2026)

Q: New

Spiral Gauntlet 2.0: Arc 2! The run goes on past Malistaire with a boss rush through five more bosses, ten tiers in all:

Tier 6, Celestia: Mithraya. Tier 7, Grizzleheim: Belloq. Tier 8, Avalon: the Jabberwock. Tier 9, Azteca: a random boss in Xibalba. Tier 10: Morganthe.

Each arc 2 tier goes straight from the shops to its boss. Bosses give the experience for the next tier (to level 60, 70, 80, 90, 100 and 110) and 100,000 gold, and from Malistaire on battles give experience and gold at x1.

Arc 2's bosses cheat as in the real game (Niduus's boss cheats, v1.0.133).

The launcher's map shows all ten tiers.

Q: Changed

Versions now count up from 2.0.0.

# v1.0.134 (October 25, 2026)

Q: Fixed

Shops for wizards above level 100: the stock was only ever level 100, so the deck shop had nothing to swap in and sold its own Novice decks. Wizards above the highest stock level now shop as a level 100 wizard (levels 90 to 100), and a slot with little in that range (decks) adds the highest pieces just below it.

RockHammer's jewel shop: jewels from levels 95 to 100 for high wizards (not just level 100), at least 30 of them, and every shape in turn, so square, circle, triangle and tear jewels (damage, pierce, accuracy...) show up next to the pins instead of pins taking most of the shop.

# v1.0.133 (October 25, 2026)

Q: New

Boss cheats! Arc 2 bosses cheat like they do in the real game: free spells, lines over the boss, second phases. Mithraya smoke-screens you, Belloq, the Jabberwock and Morganthe have their own tricks, and Xibalba's random boss cheats too if it has any.

The cheats are Niduus's: 517 creature files from his fork, in spiraldb-extra/BossCheats (one editable JSON file per creature, with a README). Anyone can write or change a boss's cheats there and send them in.

Cheats only work from tier 6 on, so arc 1 plays as before. Settings has "Boss cheats" (on or off) and "Boss cheats from tier".

Some cheats need things the gauntlet doesn't do yet (a minion joining, the boss being defeated, the boss's own casts and a few more). Those are skipped and named in the server log; they come in later updates.

# v1.0.132 (October 24, 2026)

Q: Fixed

Opening the pets page could crash the game: a run pet could roll a talent with no icon (a retired or unused one), and the game fell over drawing it. Run pets only roll talents with an icon and a name now, and such talents are taken off pets you already have when you log in or change zones.

# v1.0.131 (October 24, 2026)

Q: Changed

The server log no longer lists every May Cast talent in the game as "Pet May Cast ... casts ..." when it starts, which looked like a pet casting spells it doesn't have. Real May Casts in battle are still logged ("Duel ... | Pet May Cast ... casts ... after a cast").

# v1.0.130 (October 24, 2026)

Q: Fixed

Enchantments go on the card you put them on. With two copies of the same card in hand, the server took the wrong copy out, so its hand stopped matching yours: enchants landed on another card, said "already enchanted", or did nothing (Epic, Simplify and others).

Long May Casts (Meteor Storm, Virulence and the like) play out before the next turn starts. Each May Cast now waits for its own animation instead of at most 4 seconds.

Silver chests pay out on a run: winning the lock game gives 2 rewards (gear, jewels or spells), 10,000 to 50,000 gold, and a 20% chance of a pet.

# v1.0.129 (October 24, 2026)

Q: Fixed

Gear "Extract ... Animus" cards (like Extract Gobbler) did nothing and passed your turn. Gear now gives a +100 damage enchantment (Strong) in their place, and .run cards and the equip message say so. Relog or change zones for gear you already wear.

# v1.0.128 (October 24, 2026)

Q: Changed

Boss gold (100,000) starts at tier 6's boss; tiers 1 to 5's bosses no longer give it. Settings has "Boss gold from tier".

# v1.0.127 (October 24, 2026)

Q: Fixed

Tier 9 (Xibalba): the boss no longer turns into an ordinary creature. It becomes a random boss of a similar level, with the health of Xibalba's own boss.

Creatures shown with the game's "Boss" title count as bosses everywhere on a run (they stay bosses and end tiers), and "Minion" creatures are no longer picked to stand in for street creatures.

# v1.0.126 (October 24, 2026)

Credits now thank ljste's Spiral Shuffle, which inspired this.
