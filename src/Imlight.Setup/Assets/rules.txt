# The idea

A run is a climb through tiers. Each tier is a street full of creatures, then a boss. Beat the boss, shop and train, then head into the next tier. Everything is shuffled: the creatures, their decks, the spells you can learn, the gear in the shops and the music.

# How a tier works

Q: 1. Fight on the street
Each tier starts on a street with a countdown (about 10 minutes, set for each tier). Battle as much as you can: you earn experience, gold, gear, spells, pets, jewels and stat potions. The timer only counts while you are online, and the time left shows on screen.

Q: 2. Beat the boss
When the timer runs out you are sent to the tier's boss. Win the battle against the boss to clear the tier.

Q: 3. Shop
After the boss you arrive in the Shopping District. Spend your gold on random gear, pets from the Pet Shop and jewels from RockHammer's Jewel Works. The shops restock every tier.

Q: 4. Refill your potions
Talk to Maxwell Sharp, the peacock in the Shopping District, to refill your health and mana potions.

Q: 5. Train in Ravenwood
Use the Tome of Travels (the tome on its pedestal in the Shopping District) to go to Ravenwood. The trainers there teach random spells from every school. Spend your training points wisely.

Q: 6. Start the next tier
Use the Tome of Travels again to start the next tier's street. Want to shop some more first? Talk to Mr. Lincoln in Ravenwood to go back to the Shopping District.

# Good to know

Q: Arcs
Arc 1 runs from Wizard City to Malistaire in Dragonspyre. After its last boss you can take a break, and your run waits for you.

Arc 2 is a boss rush: tiers 6 to 10 (Celestia, Grizzleheim, Avalon, Azteca and Morganthe) go straight from the shops to the boss. Each boss gives the experience for the next tier's level (up to 110) and 100,000 gold, and arc 2's bosses cheat like they do in the real game.

Q: One life?
With Die = Delete on (under Run options), a wizard who dies in battle and is still dead when the battle ends is gone for good. Fleeing doesn't count.

Q: Stat potions
Won battles often give a stat potion that is drunk on the spot: a random stat (health, mana, damage, resist, critical and more) goes up for the rest of the run.

Q: Your stats
The Crown Shop button shows your stats in chat during a run (there are no crowns on a run).

Q: Gear cards
On a run, the cards your gear gives are swapped for random ones of the same type and pip cost (the gear's tooltip still shows its own). When you put gear on, chat tells you what its cards really are.

Q: Handy commands
Type .run status to see where your run stands, .run stats for your stats, and .run cards for what your gear's cards are on this run.
